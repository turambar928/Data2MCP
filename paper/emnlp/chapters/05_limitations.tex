\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Two limitations are worth noting. First, although we mitigate run-to-run variability by evaluating each configuration multiple times and reporting averaged scores, frontier LLM evaluation remains costly; this constrains how many repeats we can afford and limits the precision of variance estimates. Second, our strategy catalog is automatically extracted and thus contains unevenly useful entries; in practice, the router concentrates on a small subset of strategies, so this primarily affects selection efficiency and interpretability rather than the viability of strategy injection itself.

Our system is intended for research evaluation and may produce plausible but incorrect analyses. If used in real settings, such errors could lead to over-trust and misguided decisions. In addition, our experiments rely on closed-source LLM APIs, which introduces external dependencies (availability, pricing, and policy changes) and can limit strict reproducibility. We mitigate these risks by (i) encouraging users to verify conclusions against underlying evidence, (ii) logging tool traces and intermediate outputs for auditability, and (iii) using validator components to check compliance with the injected methodology and basic output consistency; we do not claim the system is suitable for high-stakes deployment without additional safeguards and human oversight.
